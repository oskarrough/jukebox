Rack devices in series

				

				

					Guide
					

						The format started as Simon Willison's Scrimshaw. A song is a
						head, some voices, some patterns and a loop line. Anything after # is a comment.
					

					
Head

					

						title, about, composer, tempo 120, beats 4 (beats to a bar),
						steps 4 (steps to a beat), swing 0.1 (0 to 0.75, delays every other step), reverb 0.3 (0
						to 1). key G or key Dm sets the song's key: the key switch transposes what you wrote, loop bricks land
						in that key, and new songs start in whatever key is selected.
					

					
Notes

					

						With beats 4 and steps 4, each bar holds 16 steps, and every bar has to add up to exactly that; the
						error says which bar is off. Each word is a step. A note like A4 uses one; each dash holds it a step longer, so
						A4- is two steps and A4--- four. Each dot is a step of rest, ... three. Separate bars
						with |.
					

					

						Chords: [Am7], [C/E], [D5], or stack notes yourself with C4+E4+G4.
						~D4 slides into D4 from the voice's previous note. D4! is louder, D4? softer; marks
						stack. q is a half-flat, a quarter tone down: Eq4 is the E of maqamat Rast and Bayati,
						Bq3 the B of Rast, and ~ slides into quarter tones too.
					

					

						Alone in a bar, % repeats the previous bar, _ leaves it silent and = holds the last note
						through it. Two lines for the same voice in one pattern run on: the second line's bars follow the first's.
					

					
Drums

					

						Drum lanes are one character a step: x a hit, X an accent, o a soft hit, . a
						rest. Spaces don't count, so x... x... x... x... is a bar of four on the floor.
					

					
Voices

					

						voice lead flute vol=0.6 pan=-0.3: a name, an instrument, then options. vol 0 to 2 (0.8);
						pan -1 left to 1 right; rev reverb send 0 to 1 (each instrument has its own);
						oct=1 shifts octaves, trans=-2 semitones; gate how much of each note sounds, 0.05 to 1
						(0.5 is staccato); glide seconds a ~ slide takes (0.07); bright 0.1 dark to 4 bright (1);
						att and rel attack and release in seconds; center=E4 where chords are voiced;
						drive 0 to 4, dirt for 808, logdrum and drive.
					

					
Patterns and the loop

					

						pattern A starts a pattern; its music lines follow, name | bar | bar |. A pattern lasts as long as its
						longest voice. pattern A tempo=90 beats=3 changes timing for that pattern alone.
					

					

						pattern B from A starts as a copy of A. A voice you write in B replaces A's whole line for that voice;
						lead@3 | … | replaces only bar 3 onwards, as many bars as you write, and keeps the rest.
						mute hat snare
						drops voices.
					

					

						loop A A2 B*2 C+5 repeats forever: *2 plays a pattern twice, +5 or
						-2 transposes it. Put play intro before it to play the intro once first; a pattern called
						intro on the loop line repeats with everything else. Games join most songs mid-loop, since a radio doesn't wait for
						you, so the loop has to stand on its own and the intro is a treat for whoever hears the start. With no play or
						loop, every pattern loops in order.
					

					
Placing it in a game

					

						In NK GTA, street Sonnenallee in the head makes the song the song of that street: Spätis, bars, windows and cars
						there usually play one of its songs. at doener cafe does the same for kinds of place: spaeti,
						bar, doener, imbiss, cafe, and feld for a wide open field like
						Tempelhof. at autoradio puts the song on your own car radio: it always runs once you sit in a car, and plays its
						songs one after another. Konvoi belongs to the wedding convoy alone and plays nowhere else. at takes any words;
						kinds the game doesn't know yet, like markt for a market, wait harmlessly until it does.
					

					

						Links start playing straight away: ?song=konvoi&kind=convoy&d=20. The address bar always shows what is
						playing.
					

					
Instruments

					

						Keys and mallets: rhodes tine electric piano, organ, pipe organ,
						harpsichord, accordion, steeldrum, marimba, vibes,
						celesta, glock, xylophone, musicbox, bell,
						timpani.
					

					

						Strings: strings section, fiddle, pizz, harp, guitar,
						drive overdriven electric guitar, wah funk guitar through a wah, banjo, oud,
						saz bağlama, brighter and shorter than the oud, qanun zither.
					

					

						Winds and brass: flute, ney reed flute, clarinet, oboe,
						bassoon, brass section, trumpet, horn, tuba.
					

					

						Voices: choir sings ah, voices a soft oo pad, chop one clipped sung syllable, cut at the
						end of the note.
					

					

						Bass: 808 sliding sub, logdrum amapiano log drum, ebass picked electric bass,
						fretless, upright, synbass.
					

					

						Synths: moog mono lead, made for slides, pad, chip, cowbell808 the 808
						cowbell played as notes (C#5 is its stock pitch), and plain sine, square,
						saw, triangle.
					

					

						Drums: kick, snare, rim, clap, hat, ohat open hat,
						ride, crash, tomlo, tommid, tomhi, shaker,
						tamb, cowbell, clave, block, guiro, tri,
						conga, tumba, bongo, bongolo, timbale, cuica samba
						friction drum (X sings high, o low), bodhran, dum and tek for
						the darbuka, riq, gong, and two weathers: thunder and surf.
