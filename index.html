					Guide
					

						The format is Simon Willison's
						Scrimshaw; his guide covers voices, patterns and drum tracks. On
						top of that:
					

					

						With beats 4 and steps 4, each bar holds 16 steps. A note like A4 uses one step;
						A4--- holds for four. Each dot is a step of rest. Separate bars with |; a bar containing only
						% repeats the previous bar, and _ leaves a whole bar silent. Drum lanes use x for a hit,
						X for an accent, o for a soft hit and . for a rest.
					

					

						Put play intro before loop A B to play the intro once, then repeat A and B. A pattern called
						intro on the loop line repeats with everything else.
					

					

						q is a half-flat, a quarter tone down: Eq4 is the E of maqamat Rast and Bayati, Bq3 the B
						of Rast. Glissandi with ~ slide into quarter tones too.
					

					

						key G or key Dm in the head sets the song's key. The key switch transposes what you wrote; loop bricks
						land in that key. New songs start in whatever key is selected.
					

					

						In NK GTA, street Sonnenallee in the head makes the song the song of that street: Spätis, bars, windows and cars
						there usually play one of its songs. at doener cafe does the same for kinds of place: spaeti,
						bar, doener, imbiss, cafe, and feld for a wide open field like
						Tempelhof. at autoradio puts the song on your own car radio: it always runs once you sit in a car, and plays its
						songs one after another. Konvoi belongs to the wedding convoy alone and plays nowhere else.
					

					

						Links start playing straight away: ?song=konvoi&kind=convoy&d=20. The address bar always shows what is
						playing.
					

					

						Instruments: oud, qanun, ney, 808, drive for the overdriven
						electric guitar, ebass for the picked electric bass; drums dum and tek for the darbuka,
						riq for the tambourine; rhodes for the tine electric piano, moog for the analog mono
						lead, and cuica for the samba friction drum.
